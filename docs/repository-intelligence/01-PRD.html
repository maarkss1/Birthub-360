1. Product Overview

Name: AtlasGR

Description: Commercial Intelligence OS & B2B Prospector. A comprehensive platform integrating CRM, AI-driven prospecting (SDR voice & email), lead enrichment, and autonomous workflow orchestration.

2. Target Audience & Personas

  • Sales Representatives (SDRs/BDRs): Needs to prospect, qualify leads, and manage pipeline efficiently.
  • Sales Managers: Needs visibility into team performance, pipeline health, and revenue forecasting.
  • RevOps / Admin: Needs to configure integrations, webhooks, security policies, and AI agent behaviors.

4. Business Rules & Compliance

  • Data Enrichment Caching: Strict 24-hour cache window (via enrichedAt on Company model) to minimize third-party API costs. (IMPLEMENTADO)
  • Tenant Isolation: Enforced via PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies based on organizationId. (IMPLEMENTADO)
  • Public Endpoints: Production endpoints cannot resolve to localhost or 127.0.0.1 to prevent SSRF and configuration leaks. (IMPLEMENTADO)
