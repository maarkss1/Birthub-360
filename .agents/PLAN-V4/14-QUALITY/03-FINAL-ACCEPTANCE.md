# V4 Final Acceptance

## Product

- three-pillar positioning is explicit;
- primary navigation is simplified;
- contextual capabilities replace unnecessary top-level areas;
- Copilot is integrated into CRM and Prospecção;
- Workspace defines persona experience.

## Architecture

- 101 violations have measured burn-down;
- Intelligence has canonical boundary;
- CORE domains are consolidated;
- no known duplicate AI surface remains as a competing product entry.

## Security

- P0 closed or explicitly excepted;
- tenant isolation verified;
- legacy leakage cases closed;
- Git/PII decision documented.

## Operations

- WhatsApp replica-safe strategy;
- unified opt-out;
- resilient voice integration;
- backup/restore evidence;
- DR evidence;
- SLO/SLI.

## ML

Either:
- production-ready with data/model evidence;
or:
- explicitly blocked due to insufficient data.

## Final product principle

The platform may become more powerful without becoming more complicated for the customer.
